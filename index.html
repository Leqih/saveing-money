Penny Round

Turn everyday spending into a card game. Spend less outside your allowance, keep your rule cards unbroken, and your multiplier climbs.

  1. Skip ahead. Press the side keys on the phone to skip setup and flip between Home, Ledger and Deck.
  2. Log a spend. Tap + Log; the preview shows which card it would break before you save.
  3. Play the night. Tap End today and watch your cards go up against the multiplier.
  4. Spend stars. Deck › Shop is always open.

Your progress is saved in this browser. Settings › Start over clears it.
